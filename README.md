# Overt Psyops 3-D Maze by Tommy Richards (@TLTHE5TH)

A two-floor 3D browser labyrinth and discernment adventure built with Three.js and WebAudio, pairing dark corridor exploration with interactive chambers inspired by **#OvertPsyops** and **OvertPsyops.AI**.

## Overview

Navigate pitch-black upper and basement corridors, locate the flashlight, test claims and appearances, gather all **7 Greek letters of ΑΛΗΘΕΙΑ (ἀλήθεια / truth)**, and unlock the **OvertPsyops.AI (@TLTHE5TH)** Sanctuary door.

### Key Features

* **Two-Floor Labyrinth**
  * **Floor 1 (Upper Dungeon):** Features overt and covert wall plaques, spiderweb barriers that burn away under your flashlight beam, patrolling red-eyed spiders, a secret lever-opened passage, and the Sanctuary exit.
  * **Floor 2 (Basement):** Reached via the weak floor tile near the flashlight; contains coffins, ditches, shovels, a wall-phasing ghost, the final **Α** letter, a basement lever, and stairs back up once unlocked.
* **Interactive Chambers & Mechanics**
  * **Moth Chamber:** Five glowing lamps attract moths; only the signed, verifiable lamp with a checkable reason leads forward without draining battery.
  * **Suppression Corridor & Nobodies:** Entering the corridor drops reach sharply, dims light, and blurs the minimap; rescuing lantern-bearing Nobodies restores reach, widens light, and can unlock the **Lightbearer** badge.
  * **Conscience Mirror:** Tests honest self-assessment (`συνείδησις`) over prideful/flattering answers.
  * **λόγος (Logic) Terminal:** Presents valid and invalid statement puzzles from a 20-question bank; wrong answers summon a spider.
  * **Parable Doors & Acts Sharing Room:** Rewards discerning story meaning (`παραβολαί`) and sharing 20% battery in the spirit of **Acts 4:32** (`κοινά`) with a larger battery cache and the **Generous** badge.
  * **Toll Gate of Greed & Health Pantry:** Exposes costly dead-end shortcuts (**Unbought** badge) and contrasts short-lived "Energy Blast" spikes that crash later against steady real food.
  * **Absurdity Hall & Filter Gallery:** Lets you laugh at absurd claims (`H` or room choice) to scatter nearby spiders and inspect `#SpirituallySmartArt` filters (`Normal`, `Invert`, `Red Rainbow`, `Edge Glow`).
* **Replayability & Progression**
  * **Levels:** Choose **Level 1: Awareness**, **Level 2: Discernment**, or **Level 3: Agent** (which adds fake verification tags to covert plaques).
  * **Seeds:** Play the shared **Daily Maze**, a **Random Seed**, or enter a **Custom Seed**.
  * **Cards, Badges & Share Card:** Collect Greek Word Cards (`ἀλήθεια`, `φῶς`, `λόγος`, `πίστις`, `συνείδησις`, `κοινά`) and earn badges saved in `localStorage`, then generate a shareable escape image card for **@TLTHE5TH** and **#OvertPsyops**.

## Controls

* **Move:** `W A S D` or `Arrow Keys`
* **Look / Turn:** `Mouse` (pointer lock) or `Q` / `E`
* **Pull Lever / Interact:** `L` or `E`
* **Toggle Flashlight:** `F`
* **Laugh (Scatter Nearby Spiders):** `H`
* **Mute / Unmute Audio:** `M`
